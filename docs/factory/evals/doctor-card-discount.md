# doctor-card-discount

Show the discount percentage on the doctor card.

This is a small UI change. The card already shows the fee. When a discount is set, show that percentage on the card. When no discount is set, show no percentage.

Do not use a real person's name, number, or history.

Acceptance:

- The card shows the discount percentage when one is set.
- A card with no discount does not show a percentage.
